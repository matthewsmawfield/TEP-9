## Corrected scope

For TEP-9, field existence and universal matter coupling are imported premises of the companion programme. The new claim is the field's outer-Solar-System domain configuration and the response of the measured objects. Failure of one comet-sector prediction constrains that proposed response; it is not a standalone inference about whether the field exists.

This distinction changes the scientific argument. The paper now opens with the companion foundation and proceeds to the outer-system observables. It no longer asks the comet catalogue alone to re-establish the whole programme. The negative controls remain because they determine which boundary model is supported.

This preserves the companion framework while respecting the detailed scope of the available solution.

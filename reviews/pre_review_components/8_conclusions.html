7. Conclusions

Two dynamically distinct populations show localized, non-planetary orbital structure in the same 60° sector of inertial sky. The resident extreme-TNO alignment and the comet reconstruction channel remain significant under their stated selection and null controls, and the transit anomaly is certified as a property of the astrometric record rather than of any reduction pipeline: an independent two-leg refit of the raw MPC astrometry, built without Warsaw-lineage input, reproduces the per-comet boundary rotation ($\rho = +1.00$) and retains the registered in-cap excess. The independent aphelion channel gives post-2017 $d_\parallel=+0.095$ ($p=0.00215$) and a nominal two-era Fisher combination of $p=1.83\times10^{-6}$ with a finite Monte Carlo floor. The bidirectional machinery reproduces the second catalogue's boundary legs, but the rotation-to-time conversion is only an equivalent orbital phase scale: it is not a measured proper-time lapse without a specified TEP field, matter response, and observation model.

The character of the anomaly is the discriminant, but the channels have different evidential status. The resident-population tests and perturber insertions constrain conventional alternatives; the bidirectional rotation residual is a reduced standard-dynamics proxy rather than a direct observed-minus-model residual, and the converted “slip” is an equivalent orbital time scale. The direct catalogue-minus-simulation comparison is cap-null in the audit, so claims of a dynamical failure must remain conditional. The data are compatible with a spatially varying temporal response, but a TEP claim still requires a derived field/trajectory/measurement model and a calibrated selection-function null.

The evidence is deliberately bounded. The two-population Fisher ledger is now $p = 2.55\times10^{-4}$ from the exact source values, but it is nominal and does not remove shared selection or catalogue-lineage dependence. The first out-of-lineage reconstruction cohort fails the registered declared-axis prediction — a reversal certified as real modern astrometry by the independent leg-separated refit — yet its own dominant structure is a lapse-slip anomaly of the identical signature class, energy-flat and single-leg-localized, sitting at the opposite polarity of the same frame-anchored bipolar axis (joint opposite-polarity statistic $p = 5\times10^{-5}$; epoch ordering extremal at $p = 0.004$ under transition-refitting permutation). The independent aphelion channel survives with post-2017 $d_\parallel=+0.095$ ($p=0.00215$) and the nominal era combination quoted above. Independent DES and OSSOS tests provide useful controls, including a null where their own selection function predicts one. These results make the spatial and reconstruction channels more sharply defined — two eras reading opposite polarities of one bipolar boundary structure — not mutually independent proofs of a TEP field. What remains open is the derived field model itself: a specified TEP trajectory and matter response tested on held-out data with a calibrated discovery-selection null.

The decisive test is imminent. The Vera C. Rubin Observatory's LSST will discover thousands of detached-class objects: the domain-boundary reading predicts $\sim 59$ per cent fall inside the pre-declared 60° cap about $\bar\varpi = 49^\circ$, against a footprint-only baseline of $\sim 42$ per cent and a footprint-predicted direction of $10.5^\circ$. The discriminating gap is decisive at achievable sample sizes: $\sim 150$ new detached discoveries separate the two models at 95 per cent power and $\alpha = 1$ per cent on the cap-fraction channel ($\sim 200$ at $\alpha = 0.1$ per cent), and a fresh class-1-like comet cohort of $\sim 100$–$150$ suffices for the rotation channel — thresholds the survey yield will cross within its first years. If the new objects organize about the footprint instead, the intrinsic-axis interpretation fails publicly and cheaply. If they land where the field says they should, the outer solar system will have yielded the first macroscopic evidence that proper time is not the passive coordinate dynamics assumes it to be.

v0.1 (Addis Ababa) — 20 September 2026 — Initial release: outer-Solar-System domain-boundary tests

- Formulated the orbital alignment anomaly of extreme trans-Neptunian objects (TNOs) as a deterministic spatial proper-time field gradient (domain boundary) under TEP rather than a shepherding planet
- Demonstrated that two dynamically distinct populations (JPL Small-Body Database detached extreme TNOs and CODE cometary catalogue) independently isolate the same 60° sector of inertial sky
- Validated per-comet boundary rotation across independent Minor Planet Center astrometry refits on 586 pre-2018 comets (rho = +0.998), retaining inbound-leg in-cap excess (p = 0.034)
- Evaluated the post-2017 prospective cohort isolating the boundary between 130 and 150 AU with opposite-polarity lapse-slip structure (joint opposite-polarity test p = 1.00×10⁻⁴)
- Performed a ten-channel global synthesis across six catalogue lineages (SBDB, DES, MPCORB, CODE, Warsaw, CometEls) yielding an omnibus statistic S_10 = 187.2 (p = 0.0118 against a 20,000-draw random-axis permutation null)
- Outlined falsification criteria and future observational tests with Rubin Observatory / LSST sky-survey cohorts
